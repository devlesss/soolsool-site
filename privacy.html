Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

SoolSool does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app keeps your learning progress on your own device and sends nothing anywhere.

This policy explains what SoolSool (“the app”) does and does not do with information. It applies to the SoolSool Android app published on Google Play by Devless Inc. (“we”).

What the app stores on your device

The app saves two things, both in the standard private storage that Android gives to an app, and both readable only by this app:

  • Your progress — for each unit: which practice items you have answered correctly, your best quiz score, and how many times you have finished that unit’s quiz.
  • One setting — whether audio auto-play is on or off.

That is the complete list. These values never leave your device, are not backed up to any server of ours, and are permanently removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android’s app settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera. The app requests no Android runtime permissions at all.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone, because it has none to sell or share.

All lesson text and all audio are bundled inside the app as ordinary files. Nothing is streamed and nothing is recorded — the app never uses a microphone and performs no speech recognition.

Purchases

SoolSool offers a single one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the remaining units. All payment is handled by Google Play. We never see, receive, or store your card number or billing details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase exists on your Google account, which is also how “restore purchase” works on a new device.

Google provides developers with the standard order records for their own sales. Google’s handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

SoolSool is a language-learning app intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also note it in the app’s Google Play listing. Older versions of the app are not retroactively changed by an update to this page.
